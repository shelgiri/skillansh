Our approach

Build on your accounting knowledge. Develop the skills to work differently.

Accounting professionals, students, and business learners already understand the importance of accurate data, repeatable processes, and careful review.

Growth Circle brings that practical mindset to digital skill development through real-world workflow analysis, guided hands-on labs, implementation reviews, and structured exploration of freelance services.

Why this approach

Start with your real work. Choose the right tool second.

Most courses teach software formulas in isolation—leaving you unsure how to use them on messy real-world spreadsheets. We do the opposite: we start with the actual repetitive work you face every month (reconciliations, data cleaning, GST/tax matching, and MIS reporting) and show you how to automate it step by step.

Whether you are a working accountant, a college student building job-ready skills, a woman restarting your career, or a business professional—you learn to apply Excel Power Query, Power BI, and safe AI directly to real business data.

The SMART Accounts Method

A five-step method for making accounting work smarter.

S

Select

Identify a repetitive accounting task.

M

Map

Understand the current process.

A

Automate

Apply suitable digital tools.

R

Review

Test and refine the workflow.

T

Transform

Apply the learning to professional development and service exploration.

The method provides a practical structure for experimentation and improvement. It does not remove the need for accounting judgement, data protection, reconciliation, or human review.
