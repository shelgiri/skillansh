Excel & Power Query · By Girish Shelar

Why VLOOKUP Fails on Monthly GSTR-2B Reconciliations (And How Power Query Solves It in 1 Click)

Every month around the 14th, accountants and GST executives spend hours matching the Purchase Register exported from Tally Prime or Busy against the GSTR-2B Excel sheet downloaded from the GST portal. When you rely on manual VLOOKUP or XLOOKUP, two common problems break your sheet:

1. Invoice Number Formatting Differences: Your supplier uploads INV-2026-0045 on the portal, while your accounts assistant entered INV/2026/45 in Tally. VLOOKUP immediately returns #N/A even though the GSTIN and amount match.
2. Multi-rate Invoices: A single invoice with both 12% and 18% items appears as multiple rows in GSTR-2B, causing duplicate lookup errors.

💡 The Automation Fix: Using Excel Power Query, you can group invoices by Supplier GSTIN + Cleaned Invoice Number (automatically stripping slashes, hyphens, and leading zeros) and merge both tables in 3 seconds. Next month, you simply drop the new file into a folder and click Refresh All.
MIS Reporting & Power BI · By Girish Shelar

Building a 1-Page Executive MIS & MSME 45-Day Ageing Dashboard in Power BI

Business owners and CFOs rarely have time to read a 15-tab Excel workbook. When presenting monthly MIS (Management Information System) reports—or pitching your freelance accounting services to SME founders—clarity wins over complexity.

Especially after the introduction of Income Tax Section 43B(h) (which disallows deductions for payments to Micro and Small Enterprises delayed beyond 15/45 days), tracking creditor and debtor ageing buckets (0–30, 31–45, 46–90, and 90+ days) has become a boardroom priority.

💡 The MIS Upgrade: Connect your Tally/Excel sales and purchase registers directly to Power BI Desktop to create a visual 1-page Executive Dashboard showing Cash Flow, Gross Margin Trends, and an instant red-flag list of MSME invoices approaching the 45-day mark.
AI & Data Privacy · By Girish Shelar

How CAs, Tax Practitioners & Students Can Use AI Safely Without Exposing Client PAN/GSTIN

AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude can write complex Excel formulas, generate Power Query M-code, and draft structured replies to GST scrutiny notices in seconds. However, pasting real client names, PANs, GSTINs, or bank statements into public AI chats violates client confidentiality and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act principles.

💡 The Data-Masking Rule: Always describe the structure of your columns (e.g., “Column A has 15-digit GSTIN, Column B has Invoice Date, Column C has Taxable Value”) and use placeholders like [TAXPAYER_A] and [AMOUNT_X] when drafting tax notice replies. You get 100% of the AI productivity benefit with 0% client data risk.
