Our working framework

A five-step method for making accounting work smarter.

The SMART Accounts Method provides a structured way to move from identifying repetitive work to testing an improved workflow.

S

Select

Identify a repetitive accounting task that is suitable for improvement.

M

Map

Break down the existing process, understand the inputs and outputs, and identify where manual work occurs.

A

Automate

Apply suitable Excel, Power Query, or AI-assisted techniques to build a practical workflow.

R

Review

Test the result, inspect the output, and refine the workflow. Accounting accuracy and appropriate human review remain essential.

T

Transform

Use the practical work to develop your digital skills, strengthen your professional profile, and explore a clearly defined freelance service.

Start with one real accounting problem. Build a practical solution. Review it before moving forward.
