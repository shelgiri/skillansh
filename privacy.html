Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Skillansh ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website skillansh.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you contact us through our website.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit our contact enquiry form, learner requirements form, or live workshop registration form, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address and/or WhatsApp phone number
  • Current profile / profession and selected learning topics or skills
  • Your workflow challenge or enquiry message
  • Submission date and time

We do not collect passwords, bank account details, client PAN/GSTIN numbers, or confidential client financial information. Please do not include such sensitive third-party data in your messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about the Accounting Automation Clarity Call or the 30-day program
  • Schedule follow-up communication regarding program suitability
  • Improve our services and website experience

4. Data Storage & Security

Submitted enquiries are stored in a secure database. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and access controls.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, and form processing. These services are bound by their own privacy policies. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies for functionality and form session management. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the Contact page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
